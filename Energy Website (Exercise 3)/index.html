Appliance Energy Consumption Website

Household appliances make up a large share of electricity use in Australian homes. TVs, fridges, heaters and washing machines all add to the bill, and small changes in how we use them can reduce both cost and emissions.

This site is a short demo about appliance energy use in the Australian market. Later pages will look more closely at televisions and how energy labels help shoppers compare models.

Why it matters

Energy prices and climate targets mean households are paying more attention to how much power appliances draw. Star ratings and the Energy Rating Label make it easier to pick efficient products when shopping in Australia.
