Televisions

Televisions are common in Australian living rooms and can use a noticeable amount of electricity, especially larger screens or models left on standby.

What affects TV energy use?

  • Screen size (bigger panels usually draw more power)
  • Display type (for example LED vs OLED)
  • Brightness and picture settings
  • Hours of use each day
  • Standby power when the TV is “off” but still plugged in
